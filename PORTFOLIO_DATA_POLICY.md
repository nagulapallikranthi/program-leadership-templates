# Portfolio Data Policy

## Purpose

This policy ensures that every public artifact in this repository is generic, fictional, reusable, and free of organizational data.

## Prohibited content

Do not add:

- Employer, customer, vendor, product, service, team, or employee names
- Real Jira URLs, project keys, issue IDs, board IDs, sprint names, field IDs, or automation IDs
- Real email addresses, usernames, groups, tenant IDs, endpoints, credentials, secrets, or tokens
- Internal incidents, vulnerabilities, root-cause details, architecture, infrastructure, or security controls
- Actual financial values, ticket volumes, SLA results, staffing data, performance data, or unreleased plans
- Workplace screenshots, exports, logs, reports, or copied internal documents

## Required content standard

All examples must use fictional names and synthetic data. Illustrative numbers must be labelled as fictional, synthetic, or demonstration-only.

## Pre-publication checks

Before adding or updating an artifact, confirm that:

- The content can be understood without access to an employer system
- All names and identifiers are fictional
- All data is synthetic
- No hidden spreadsheet sheets, comments, formulas, metadata, or image properties reveal organizational information
- No credentials, endpoints, personal information, or proprietary material appear in the file or Git history
- The artifact describes a reusable method rather than reproducing an internal document

## Example fictional environment

- Organization: Northstar Digital
- Teams: Platform Operations, Reliability, Developer Experience
- Projects: OPS, ENG, SVC
- Products: Product Alpha, Product Beta
- Users: Alex Morgan, Priya Shah, Jordan Lee

These names are fictional and intended only for demonstration.
